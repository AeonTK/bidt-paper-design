\section{Conclusion}

We introduced a two-dimensional design space---autonomy and agency, each organised into five operational levels---as an analytical frame for reasoning about agentic AI deployments in regulated contexts, and proposed six architectural tactics for navigating it, illustrated through an automated funding program matching tool to support citizen-driven innovation.

Treating autonomy and agency as explicit, jointly-considered design dimensions makes visible the choices that determine who is responsible for a decision, what consequences that decision can have, whether errors can be corrected before they propagate, and where the audit trail is preserved or interrupted. The six tactics are not merely performance optimisations; they are mechanisms whose selection and combination determine how oversight, reversibility, and accountability are distributed between human and system~\cite{Lange2025}. In regulated contexts, making these choices deliberate and principled is a prerequisite for trustworthy deployment.
