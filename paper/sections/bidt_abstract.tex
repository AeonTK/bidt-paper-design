Deploying agentic AI in regulated contexts requires navigating two coupled design dimensions: \emph{autonomy}---how much human involvement a workflow requires---and \emph{agency}---what the system can perceive and affect. At higher autonomy, human error correction is less available, so reliable operation requires constraining agency; compliance reinforces this coupling.

We introduce a two-dimensional design space organising both dimensions into five operational levels, and propose six architectural tactics---checkpoints, escalation, multi-agent delegation, tool provisioning, tool fencing, and write staging---for navigating it. The tactics are demonstrated on an automated funding program matching tool for citizen-driven innovation.
